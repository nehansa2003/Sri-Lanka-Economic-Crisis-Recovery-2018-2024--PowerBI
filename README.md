# Sri Lanka Economic Crisis & Recovery | 2018–2024

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents a data-driven analysis of **Sri Lanka's economic crisis and recovery from 2018 to 2024** using Microsoft Power BI.

The dashboard analyses key economic indicators including **inflation, cost of living, food and fuel prices, exchange rates, exports and imports, tourism, workers' remittances, GDP growth, unemployment, and interest rates**.

The main objective is to identify important economic trends, understand the impact of the economic crisis, and examine the signs of economic stabilization and recovery during 2023–2024.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyse Sri Lanka's economic trends from 2018 to 2024.
* Examine inflation and changes in the cost of living.
* Analyse essential food and fuel price movements.
* Study exchange-rate fluctuations and external-sector pressures.
* Analyse exports, imports, tourism, and workers' remittances.
* Examine GDP growth and unemployment.
* Analyse monetary conditions and interest-rate movements.
* Present the findings through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## 📌 Data Sources

The raw data were collected from the following sources:

1. **Department of Census and Statistics (DCS), Sri Lanka – Data Hub**
2. **Central Bank of Sri Lanka (CBSL)**
3. **Lanka Calculator – Historical Fuel Prices**

The raw datasets were downloaded from these sources and then processed according to the requirements of the analysis.

---

## 🔄 Data Preparation Process

The data preparation process followed these main steps:

```text
Raw Data
   ↓
Download Datasets
   ↓
Examine Data
   ↓
Extract Required Columns & Records
   ↓
Remove Unnecessary Data
   ↓
Clean & Standardise Data
   ↓
Create Six Prepared Datasets
   ↓
Import into Power BI
   ↓
Create Dashboard & Analysis
```

The main preprocessing activities included:

* Selecting relevant columns and records.
* Removing unnecessary data.
* Handling missing or blank values where required.
* Removing duplicate or irrelevant records.
* Standardising column names.
* Converting data into appropriate data types.
* Standardising dates, years, months, and quarters.
* Checking numerical values and measurement units.
* Preparing the datasets for Power BI analysis.

## 📊 Power BI Dashboard

The final dashboard is titled:

**Sri Lanka Economic Crisis & Recovery | 2018–2024**

The dashboard contains four main analytical sections:

### 1. Executive Overview

Provides an overall view of Sri Lanka's economic conditions using KPI cards, GDP growth, inflation, unemployment, exchange rates, and a crisis timeline.

### 2. Inflation & Cost of Living Pressure

Analyses inflation, essential food prices, fuel prices, and changes in the cost of living.

### 3. External Sector & Economic Pressure

Examines exchange rates, exports, imports, tourism, tourist arrivals, and workers' remittances.

### 4. Economic Growth, Employment & Monetary Conditions

Analyses GDP, GDP growth, unemployment, policy interest rates, and market interest rates.
